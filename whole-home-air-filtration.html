The Benefits

Cleaner Air in Every Room on the System

Whole-home filtration pulls contaminants out of the air you breathe indoors, not just the air in one corner of the living room. The media traps mold spores, pet dander, pollen, and other fine particles that a thin grille filter lets pass.

Homeowners usually notice the difference in a few ordinary places: less dust on furniture, fewer allergy and asthma triggers in the air, and cooking, pet, or smoke smells that do not linger the way they used to. Dust and debris that used to load the coil also put less stress on the blower, which helps the heating and cooling system run the way it should.

These cabinets can be added on their own. They do their best work alongside the rest of the indoor air plan, including a properly seated air filter at the grille, a UV purification light if the coil grows mold, and an ionizer if fine dust is still the complaint. Pairing filtration with routine dryer vent cleaning also cuts one of the more common sources of airborne lint in Florida homes.
