Your One-Time Visit

What Our Technicians Check During Your One-Time Visit

A typical single HVAC maintenance appointment covers the core tasks that keep systems running safely and efficiently:

1

Thermostat and Controls

We verify settings and accuracy so the system keeps you comfortable when you are home and saves energy when you are away. System controls and the starting cycle are also checked to confirm proper operation.

2

Electrical Connections and Motors

Technicians tighten electrical connections and measure voltage and current on motors. Faulty connections can cause unsafe operation and reduce the life of major components.

3

Moving Parts and Lubrication

Lubrication of moving parts reduces friction and lowers electricity use.

4

Condensate Drain and Airflow

The condensate drain is inspected to help prevent water damage and humidity problems. Blower components are cleaned and adjusted for proper airflow. ENERGY STAR notes that airflow problems alone can reduce system efficiency by up to 15 percent.

5

Refrigerant and Coils

Refrigerant level is checked and adjusted when needed. Coils are cleaned as required so dirt does not force the unit to work harder.
