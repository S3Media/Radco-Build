Common Problems We Fix

Recognizing These Frequent Microwave Issues?

Microwaves develop problems over time from normal use, humidity, or worn components. If any of these sound familiar, reach out to Radco right away:

1

No Heating or Weak Heating

When food stays cold or warms unevenly, the problem often lies with the magnetron, high-voltage capacitor, diode, or related parts. We identify the fault and perform the repairs needed to restore strong, consistent heating. This is one of the most common reasons homeowners in Spring Hill and Brooksville call us for microwave repair.

2

Turntable Not Spinning

A stalled turntable leads to spotty cooking. Our pros examine the motor, coupler, and supporting components to ensure smooth, even rotation for better results.

3

Unresponsive Control Panel

Touchpads or buttons that do not react, or displays showing errors, usually point to electronic issues. We troubleshoot the panel and replace faulty elements to bring controls back to life.

4

Sparking or Arcing Inside

Sparks during use create safety concerns and may stem from a damaged waveguide cover, stirrer, or other internals. We locate the source and eliminate it to keep things safe.

5

Strange or Loud Noises

Buzzing, grinding, or rattling sounds often come from a worn fan motor, turntable drive, or bearings. We pinpoint the cause and quiet operation with targeted fixes.
