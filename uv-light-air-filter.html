The Benefits

Fresher Air and a Coil That Stays Cleaner

Indoor air can carry higher pollutant levels than the air outside, and most of the day is spent inside. In a Florida system, the coil stays wet for months, which is where mold, bacteria, and musty startup smells take hold.

Radco installs Second Wind UV purification systems. The benefits homeowners notice are straightforward and include fresher air, fewer airborne irritants, less mold growth on the coil, fewer lingering odors, and an air handler that does not have to push air across a filmed-over coil. A photo-catalytic process on these systems also helps break down volatile organic compounds from paints, cleaners, and furnishings.

Second Wind UV air treatment devices carry FDA Class II medical device registration. With a prescription from your doctor, you may be able to submit the cost to your insurance company, but ask your insurer before you count on it.

Our Core HVAC UV Light Services

UV Light for Existing Systems

Already have an air conditioner or heat pump? We open the indoor unit, check the cold coil and the pan that catches its water, and install a Second Wind UV lamp where that moisture sits. If the coil is already coated with dirt and mold, we clean it before the lamp goes in. A light aimed at a dirty coil cannot do its job.

Add UV to My Current System

UV Light With a New Installation

A new air conditioner still makes the coil inside the air handler cold and wet. That moisture is normal, and it is also where dust sticks and mold starts, even on equipment that was just installed. Adding the UV light during installation keeps that coil cleaner from the first cooling season and saves a separate visit later.

Lamp Check and Bulb Replacement

A UV bulb can still glow after it has gotten too weak to keep the coil clean. The useful light fades with hours of use, long before the lamp looks dark or burned out. We can check it during a service or maintenance visit and replace it when it is no longer doing the job, so you are not paying for a light that is only glowing.
