Two Ways to Finance

RTO Financing Options from RTO National

We offer two convenient pathways through RTO National, so you can choose the option that best fits your credit situation, budget, and ownership goals.

Option 1

1. RTO Lease Program

The lease program functions as a month-to-month rental agreement with an option to own, making it ideal if you want to avoid credit checks.

No Credit Needed: Approval is practically guaranteed for applicants who meet basic requirements.
How It Works: RTO National purchases the equipment from Radco and leases it to you.
No Penalty Buyout: You can pay off the remaining balance at any time to take full ownership with no penalty.

Best for: Homeowners who prefer flexibility or want to avoid a traditional credit check.

Apply for RTO Lease Now

Option 2

2. Traditional Financing

Traditional financing operates as an installment loan rather than a rental agreement.

Credit Check Required: You must submit an application and undergo a credit check to secure an approval and an interest rate.
Fixed Terms: You borrow the money for a set period (e.g., 24 to 60 months) with a fixed, simple-interest Annual Percentage Rate (APR).
Immediate Ownership: You own the unit from day one, and RTO National (or their partners) sets a fixed monthly payment schedule until the loan is completely paid off.

Best for: Homeowners with qualifying credit who want to own the system outright from day one.

The Details

Getting Started & Daily Mechanics

Upfront Costs: Both programs generally require an initial payment to Radco on the day you sign, which may include your first month’s payment, sales tax, and an administrative fee.
Optional Damage Protection: RTO National offers a Liability Damage Waiver (LDW) on leases, which releases you from payment liabilities if the item is destroyed by fire, flood, theft, or severe storms.
Managing Payments: Ongoing monthly payments are made directly to RTO National, typically via their RTO National Payment Center portal.
The Case for Financing

Why Financing Your Air Conditioner Makes Sense in Hernando & the Surrounding Counties

Many homeowners wait longer than they would like to replace an aging unit because of the cost. Flexible financing changes that timeline. When your current system struggles during peak summer heat, the discomfort and higher energy use add up quickly. The U.S. Department of Energy notes that air conditioners account for about 12% of electricity use in U.S. households, which is why upgrading to a properly installed, efficient system can deliver meaningful long-term value.

Spreading the cost through financing lets you capture those benefits sooner while keeping payments manageable. Local factors matter too. Hernando County and other West Central Florida homes often face high humidity alongside heat, so a well-chosen and correctly installed system does more than cool — it helps control moisture and improve overall indoor comfort. Financing simply makes that upgrade more accessible for more families.
