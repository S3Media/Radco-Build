All-in-One Comfort

What Is a Package Unit and How Does It Differ from Other Systems?

A package unit houses the compressor, condenser, evaporator, and often the heating section in one weather-resistant cabinet placed outside the home — typically on a ground pad or rooftop curb. This all-in-one design simplifies installation in certain homes and can reduce the need for extensive indoor modifications.

Many homeowners in Hernando County appreciate the compact footprint and straightforward service access. In contrast, split systems separate the indoor and outdoor components, which works well when attic or closet space is available for the air handler.

To understand how package units compare with split systems for your specific situation, review our guide on AC split system installation.

Our Process

Our Professional Package Unit Installation Process

Every installation we perform follows a structured approach focused on performance, safety, and minimal disruption:

  1. 1
    In-Home Evaluation and Load Calculation: We assess your home’s layout, insulation, windows, and existing ductwork. Accurate sizing prevents short-cycling and supports both comfort and efficiency.
  2. 2
    System Selection and Planning: We recommend equipment matched to your needs, considering efficiency ratings and Florida’s climate demands. We handle permitting requirements where applicable.
  3. 3
    Site Preparation: This includes placing a level pad or preparing a roof curb, ensuring proper clearances, and protecting landscaping.
  4. 4
    Unit Placement and Connections: The package unit is set, secured, and connected to your duct system, electrical supply, and condensate drain. All connections are sealed and tested.
  5. 5
    Testing, Startup, and Education: We verify airflow, refrigerant charge if applicable, thermostat operation, and overall performance. You receive clear guidance on operating and maintaining your new system.

This methodical process helps your equipment deliver the reliable comfort you expect. For more context on general installation standards we follow across Hernando and Pasco counties, see our air conditioning services overview.
