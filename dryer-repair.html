Common Symptoms

Spotting These Typical Dryer Troubles?

Dryers develop issues over time from normal use, lint buildup, or worn components. If any of these sound familiar, reach out to Radco right away:

1

No Heat or Dryer Not Heating

When the dryer runs but stays cold, the problem often involves the heating element, thermal fuse, high-limit thermostat, or gas valve components. We identify the exact cause and restore proper heat, so clothes dry completely. This is one of the most common calls we receive for dryer repair near me and dryer not heating Spring Hill.

2

Dryer Will Not Turn On

Power failures frequently tie to wiring problems, a faulty door switch, thermal fuse, or control board issues. Our technicians inspect the electrical system and bring the unit back online safely.

3

Loud or Strange Noises

Rattling, grinding, or squealing usually comes from worn belts, bearings, rollers, or idler pulleys. We pinpoint the source and quiet the machine effectively.

4

Drum Not Rotating

If tumbling stops, it could be a snapped belt, motor failure, or pulley problem. Our team diagnoses the issue and restores smooth rotation.

5

Clothes Stay Damp Too Long

Slow drying (even with heat) frequently points to restricted airflow from a clogged lint screen or vent system. We address the mechanical problem and often recommend professional vent cleaning to restore full efficiency and reduce fire risk.
