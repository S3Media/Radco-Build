Common Problems We Fix

Spotting These Typical Oven, Range, and Stove Troubles?

Cooking appliances develop issues over time from normal use, age, or worn components. If any of these sound familiar, reach out to Radco right away:

1

Oven Not Heating or Temperature Swings

This often points to a failed heating element, faulty temperature sensor, or thermostat problem on electric models, or an igniter or gas valve issue on gas units. We identify the exact cause and restore steady, accurate heat.

2

Burners That Will Not Light or Stay Lit

Clicking without flame, weak burners, or uneven heating on a gas range or stove usually involves the igniter, spark module, or burner ports. Our technicians clean, adjust, or replace the necessary parts and confirm safe operation.

3

Control Panel Errors or Unresponsive Buttons

Blank displays, error codes, or buttons that do nothing interrupt every setting. We diagnose electronic faults, repair or replace control boards, and return full functionality.

4

Door That Will Not Seal Properly

Loose hinges, worn gaskets, or latch failures allow heat to escape, reduce efficiency, and create safety concerns. We repair or replace these components so the door seals securely.

5

Uneven Cooking or Cold Spots

Inconsistent results frequently stem from a failing element, sensor issues, or airflow problems inside the cavity. We evaluate the full system and make the targeted repairs.
