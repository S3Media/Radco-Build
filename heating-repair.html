Common Symptoms

Spotting These Typical Heating System Troubles?

Heating systems develop issues over time from normal use, humidity, or worn components. If any of these sound familiar, reach out to Radco right away:

1

Only Cold or Lukewarm Air From the Vents

This often points to a failing igniter, flame sensor, or heat exchanger problem on a furnace, or a reversing valve or refrigerant issue on a heat pump. We identify the exact cause and restore proper heat output.

2

Unusual Sounds or Smells

Rattling, grinding, or burning odors can signal loose parts, a failing blower motor, or electrical problems. Our technicians diagnose the source and complete the targeted repair before the issue worsens.

3

Unexpected Spikes in Utility Costs

A system that runs longer than usual or cycles inefficiently drives up energy bills. We locate the mechanical or airflow problem and return the unit to efficient operation.

4

Frequent Cycling On and Off

Short cycling places extra stress on components and reduces comfort. Common causes include thermostat issues, dirty filters, or restricted airflow. We correct the underlying problem so the system runs as designed.

5

Inconsistent Temperatures Across Rooms

Uneven heating frequently stems from duct problems, blower issues, or a system that is no longer properly sized or balanced. We evaluate the full system and make the necessary adjustments or repairs.
