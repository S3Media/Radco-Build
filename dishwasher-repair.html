Common Symptoms

Spotting These Typical Dishwasher Troubles?

Dishwashers develop issues over time from normal use, hard water, or worn components. If any of these sound familiar, reach out to Radco right away:

1

Subpar Cleaning Results

Dishes coming out with leftover food, spots, or film often trace back to blocked spray arms, a defective inlet valve, or pump wear. We locate the cause and restore proper scrubbing power.

2

Water Leaks

Leaks can damage floors and cabinets while disrupting kitchen routines. Our technicians identify whether the source is a worn seal, gasket, hose, or another component and make the targeted repair.

3

Odd Sounds During Operation

Rattling, grinding, or unusual humming usually points to problems with the motor, pump, or internal parts. We diagnose the origin and quiet the machine with the right fix.

4

Control Panel Failures

Unresponsive buttons or flashing error codes signal electronic or control board issues. We troubleshoot the system and replace only what is needed to regain full operation.

5

Drainage Failures

Standing water or slow draining frequently comes from clogged filters, a failing drain pump, or obstructed hoses. We clear the problem, so the dishwasher empties completely. Persistent drainage issues sometimes involve the garbage disposal as well, which is why we also offer expert garbage disposal repair throughout the Spring Hill area and beyond.
